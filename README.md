# Shopify-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Shopify store performance, covering sales, customer behaviour, retention, regional demand, payment methods and product performance.

📌** Project Overview**

**This dashboard gives a single view of how the store is performing and where growth can come from. It helps answer:**

How much revenue is the store generating, and what is the average order value?
How many customers come back after their first purchase?
Which cities, products and payment gateways drive sales?
At what times of day and on which days do sales peak?

Power BI – dashboard design and interactivity

**🔍 Key Insights**

Net sales total $681K from 1,276 units, with a high average order value of about $535.
Only 9% of customers are repeat buyers. 1,050 of 1,157 customers ordered once, giving a purchase frequency of just 1.10.
Running Shoes lead product sales at about $0.34M, roughly half of total revenue, followed by Cycling, Tennis and Walking Shoes. Other categories contribute very little.
Washington, Dallas and New York City are the top cities by sales, with El Paso and Houston close behind. Customers are spread across the whole US.
Daily sales peak early and mid-period. They start at about $115K, dip to about $87K on day 20, recover to about $107K on days 21–22, then fall to about $82K by day 24.
Orders concentrate around midday hours, with activity rising through the morning and tapering off in the evening.

**💡 Business Takeaways**

Improve retention. With a 9% repeat rate, loyalty programs, post-purchase emails and bundle offers could lift repeat purchases.
Lean into Running Shoes, while testing promotions to grow the smaller categories.
Prioritize top cities such as Washington, Dallas and New York City for targeted campaigns.
Schedule promotions and ads for midday hours, when customers are most active.
Investigate the late-period sales decline to see whether it is seasonal or campaign-driven.
